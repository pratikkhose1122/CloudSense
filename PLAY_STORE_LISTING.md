## 3. Full Description

**Introduction**
Stay ahead of the changing climate with **CloudSense**, your intelligent companion for weather and air quality monitoring. Whether you are planning your daily commute or looking out for your health, CloudSense provides precise, real-time data wrapped in a stunning, modern glassmorphism interface.

**Key Features**
🌤️ **Real-Time Weather Updates**: Get accurate current temperature, humidity, wind speed, and visibility details instantly.
😷 **Air Quality Index (AQI)**: Monitor pollutants like PM2.5, PM10, CO, and NO2 with detailed health recommendations to keep you safe.
📍 **Hyper-Local Forecasts**: Automatic location detection ensures you get the most relevant weather data for your exact area.
🔔 **Smart Notifications**: Powered by Firebase, receive instant alerts for severe weather changes or poor air quality conditions.
✨ **Modern Glassmorphism UI**: Experience a beautiful, clutter-free design that makes checking the weather a delight.

**Why Choose CloudSense?**
CloudSense isn't just another weather app. It combines accurate data sources (OpenWeather) with a user-centric design that prioritizes performance and aesthetics. It’s lightweight, fast, and built reliable with Flutter.

**Privacy & Security**
We value your privacy. CloudSense uses your location solely to provide accurate local weather data. No personal information is collected, stored, or sold to third parties.
